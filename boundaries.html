Creative freedom needs care around it.

The goal is not to make creators timid. It is to help them move with enough respect, clarity and consent that the work can travel further.

People

Ask before using someone's face, voice, body, name, private story or recognisable likeness. AI likeness is still likeness.

Music Rights

Know who owns the song, recording, samples, beats, stems and artwork. A private draft and a public release can have different risk.

Place

Some places are public to stand in but not free to film, drone, commercialise or culturally reframe.

Culture

Do not treat cultural material, ceremony, language, grief, sacred places or community memory as prompt fuel. Ask, listen and accept no.

Youth

Young creators and subjects need extra consent, privacy, safeguarding and adult review before anything becomes public.

AI Disclosure

If AI helped make images, voices, edits or scripts, record that in the project notes and decide what should be disclosed publicly.

Private Notes

Keep raw feelings, private references, contact details, health details, relationship material and sensitive source trails out of public files.

Final Call

Tools can suggest. Collaborators can advise. The creator or rights-holding group still decides what the work means and where it goes.
