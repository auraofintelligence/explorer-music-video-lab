Start with open-ended questions.

Good clips do not begin with a model name. They begin with attention: what does the song want to reveal, protect, repair, celebrate or leave unresolved?

What does the chorus ask the viewer to feel? What world appears for thirty seconds, then disappears? What should stay private, symbolic or off-camera?

Plain-language film terms

Storyboard: a simple visual plan for the main moments.

Keyframe: the important still image at the start or end of a clip.

First cut: the first watchable version, made for review rather than perfection.
