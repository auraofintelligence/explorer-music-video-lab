Markdown builders for music-video clips.

Each builder creates a plain `.md` file. Plain text travels well between creators, agents, editors, festivals and future tools.

01

Song Seed

Capture the song, audience, feeling, open question and public/private boundary.

 02

Visual Brief

Name the world, style, references, motifs, consistency rules and no-go list.

 03

Storyboard

Turn the song into a handful of still moments before making moving clips.

 04

Keyframe Shot

Describe one short shot with start frame, end frame, timing and motion.

 05

Clip Review

Approve, revise, replace or export clips with a clear human review note.

 06

Release Plan

Check audience, rights, captions, AI disclosure, festival fit and final boundaries.

Use them in order, or jump straight to the job.

The files are modular. A creator can make one quick shot prompt or build a full end-to-end release pack.
