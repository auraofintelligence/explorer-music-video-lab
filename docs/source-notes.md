## Patterns Reused

### i C. infinity Music Universe

Used for the idea of song pages, visual briefs, storyboards, keyframe shots, clip reviews and agent-ready Markdown handoffs.

### GenAI Music Video Documents

Used for the plain workflow:

1. Listen and map the song.
2. Choose a story mode.
3. Use still images and storyboard panels as cheap previsualisation.
4. Turn approved panels into start/end keyframes.
5. Generate or shoot short clips.
6. Assemble a first cut.
7. Review with a human in the loop.

### Quandamooka Film Festival

Used for the beginner-friendly film-planning doorway, AI storyboarding tone, public/private boundaries and builder pattern.

### Strange But True

Used as a neighbouring field-guide voice: practical, locally useful, open to odd sparks, and careful about not overclaiming.

### Strange But True Travel Oracle

Used for the self-sovereign framing: the model can suggest, but the explorer keeps authority.

### Strange But True Cosmic Nexus

Used for the wider explorer lens: mystery, films, source trails, public/private boundaries and connected story-worlds.

### Strange But True AI Trust Index

Used as a reminder that AI tool details change quickly and should be refreshed before being treated as current.

### P4A Musicverse

Used for the culture-as-public-imagination pattern: music, jokes, grief, repair and civic ideas can become singable before they become formal systems.
