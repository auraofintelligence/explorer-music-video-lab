# Style Guide

## Voice

Use plain Australian English.

The voice should feel like a calm creative workbench:

- practical
- optimistic
- self-sovereign
- beginner-friendly
- honest about uncertainty
- open to mystery without treating mystery as proof
- warm enough for artists
- clear enough for first-time filmmakers

## Core Promise

Explorers Music Video Lab helps a creator turn a song into a music-video clip without handing away the meaning.

The central idea is:

> Tools can help with structure, prompts, drafts and review. The creator keeps the final call.

## Audience

Write for:

- emerging musicians
- phone filmmakers
- inspirational explorers
- community storytellers
- festival entrants
- artists making first clips
- AI-curious beginners
- collaborators who need a clean handoff

Do not assume the audience knows film school or AI jargon.

## Plain-Language Terms

- Storyboard: a simple visual plan for the main moments.
- Keyframe: an important still image at the start or end of a clip.
- First cut: the first watchable edit, made for review.
- B-roll: extra supporting footage that helps the main story breathe.
- Lip-sync: matching mouth movement to the sung or spoken words.
- Handoff: a clear file that lets another person or agent continue the work.

## Copy Rules

- Use open-ended questions.
- Explain domain-specific language briefly, then move on.
- Keep the music-video purpose central.
- Avoid hype about AI replacing filmmakers.
- Avoid ranking creators, destinations, stories or cultures.
- Avoid claims that a tool guarantees quality.
- Avoid official festival or community authority claims.
- Do not turn Strange But True, P4A, i C. infinity or Quandamooka Film Festival into a single brand system.

## Visual Feel

Modern creative workbench:

- clean off-white background
- deep ink text
- teal for trust and navigation
- coral for action
- gold for insight
- calm cards and stable grids
- real music-video planning imagery
- no purple-dominated palette
- no generic SaaS dashboard look

## Builder Rules

Builder pages are first-class pages.

Each builder should:

- autosave locally
- generate readable Markdown
- allow copy and download
- include boundary notes
- keep creator authority visible
- avoid storing private details anywhere except the user's own browser

## Boundary Posture

Always separate:

- inspiration from permission
- public notes from private notes
- AI drafts from human truth
- tool suggestions from final creative authority
- cultural context from content to harvest
